I build recommendation systems. I'm a Sr. Tech-Lead Manager and AI Research Scientist at Meta, working on ranking and recommendation for Instagram. My recent work spans generative retrieval, LLM-based recommendation, and multimodal content understanding at billion-user scale.

Before Meta I led Shop Ads ranking at TikTok, ran a 30-person ads recommendation team at JD.com, built recruiter search at LinkedIn, and was a research scientist at Yahoo Labs. I hold a PhD from Northeastern University and a BS from Tsinghua University.

This site is where I keep long-form notes on papers and ideas at the intersection of recommender systems and large language models.
